Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Arrow Rush respects and protects your privacy. We do not require accounts or store personal identities.

1. Information We Collect

All core gameplay data (high scores, coin balances, skin unlocks, sound and control preferences) is stored entirely offline on your local device.

2. Third-Party Advertising (AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may automatically collect pseudonymous advertising IDs and device performance signals to serve advertisements.

3. Permissions Used

  • VIBRATE: Used solely for in-game tactile feedback. Can be disabled in Settings.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to load ads and check connectivity.

4. Children's Privacy

Arrow Rush is built for general arcade audiences (ages 10+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
