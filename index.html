Privacy Policy for Arrow Rush

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

Arrow Rush ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we do and do not collect, how local data is handled, and how third-party advertising services operate within the Arrow Rush mobile application on Android.

1. Information Collection & Use

Arrow Rush is an arcade reflex game designed to be privacy-friendly. We do NOT require any account registration, email signup, personal names, phone numbers, or passwords to play.

🎮 Local Storage Only

Your high scores, collected coins, unlocked arrow skins, equipped themes, sound/vibration toggles, and achievements are stored solely on your local Android device via private app preferences.

🚫 No Personal Profiles

We do not operate servers that log your identity, contact information, photos, files, or sensitive personal data.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Arrow Rush displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support free gameplay (including Banner, Interstitial, and Native formats). These third-party ad networks may collect and process information such as:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (GAID) and Android device model.
  • Network & Diagnostics: IP-derived coarse general location and app performance diagnostics for fraud prevention and ad measurement.

To learn how Google manages advertising data, please review Google's official documentation:

🔗 Google Privacy & Terms | AdMob Policies

3. Android Permissions Required

Our application requests only the minimum permissions necessary for gameplay and advertising:

  • VIBRATE: Used solely to generate tactile vibration pulses when matching arrows, hitting combos, or making mistakes. You can turn this off anytime in the in-game Settings.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the app to connect to Google AdMob servers to display test and live advertisements and check network connectivity.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Rush does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will promptly take appropriate action.

5. Data Retention & How to Delete Data

Because all gameplay data (scores, coins, unlocked skins) is saved exclusively on your local device, you can delete and reset all data at any time by:

  • Tapping "RESET GAME SCORES" in the in-game Settings screen, or
  • Clearing App Storage / Cache in Android Settings > Apps > Arrow Rush > Storage > Clear Storage, or
  • Uninstalling the game from your Android device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.
